--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FAST LAUNCH (NATIVE WINDOW — NO CHROME WINDOW!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Double-click ApexEngineer.exe.
- Opens immediately in its own native Windows desktop window with the
  ApexEngineer app icon (no Chrome window, no browser tabs, no console).
- On first launch on a fresh PC without Node.js, it automatically sets
  up a portable runtime inside ".nodejs" once. Every launch after that
  starts in under a second.
- To create a Desktop shortcut with the ApexEngineer icon, either:
    * Double-click Create_Desktop_Shortcut.bat, OR
    * Click the top-left icon of the ApexEngineer window ->
      "Create Desktop Shortcut", OR
    * Right-click ApexEngineer.exe -> Pin to taskbar / Send to Desktop.
